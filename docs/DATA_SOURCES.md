# Monarch Migration Live — Data Source Research

## Live/recent
- **iNaturalist API** — recent monarch occurrence records. Product filters to observation licenses CC0 and CC BY only and does not republish photos.
- **National Weather Service API** — point lookup plus hourly forecast for temperature, wind direction/speed and precipitation probability. Open U.S. government data.

## Historical
- **GBIF Occurrence API** — local occurrence history, filtered to CC0 / CC BY licenses. Counts are explicitly labeled an observation index because observer effort changes over time.

## Phenology / migration biology
- **Monarch Watch** — latitude-based peak migration table, spring arrival table, leading-edge timing and weather/coast modifiers.
- **U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service** — eastern/western population biology and long-term conservation framing.
- **Western Monarch Count / Xerces Society** — western overwintering counts and survey windows.

## Linked, not ingested
- **Journey North** — valuable current reports for adults, eggs, larvae, milkweed and roosts. Historical dataset license is noncommercial ShareAlike, so it is linked rather than republished on a monetized site without additional permission.

## Current 2026 anchors researched for v0.1
- Eastern overwintering winter 2025–26: **2.93 hectares**, versus **1.79 hectares** prior winter.
- Western 2025 mid-season count: **~12,260 monarchs across 249 sites**, third-lowest formal count since 1997.
- Western 2026 survey windows: **Oct 17–Nov 1**, **Nov 14–Dec 6**, **Dec 26–Jan 10, 2027**.

## Great Lakes evidence
Michigan field guidance and Journey North reports document monarchs concentrating and roosting along Great Lakes shorelines during fall migration. Monarch Watch has also collected reports of Great Lakes water crossings. The product uses these as context layers, not claims of live roost occupancy.
